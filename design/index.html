Design system

Light, calm and caring, taken from the client's login mockup and the Rehab Health logo. Teal is for actions. The four figure colours mark categories. Status has its own colours.

Colour

Actions use brand

brand-700 on white, 9.0 contrast. Hover goes to brand-800.

Coral and sun carry no text

Contrast is about 2:1. Use them as fills and dots, with ink-900 text on top.

Status is not brand

Emerald, amber and red are used for status only, so olive and sun stay categories.
